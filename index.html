Design tags game · round 20261110-1844

You are filling design tags for clothing products, one job after another, for the design studio LAB. Each job is one product: its photos, the shop's own text, and the catalogue of tags below. Work through every job of this round, then answer with one JSON block in the answer format.

Rules

  1. Fill only what the product's photos or its text show. Never guess and never invent: leave a part empty when it is not visible or not stated.
  2. Pick tags from the catalogue, on their path. Tags waiting for approval may be used too.
  3. A tag the catalogue lacks goes in new_tags only, with its full path (e.g. Trims and details > Drawcord > Ends > Aglet), the words the shop uses and why.
  4. Tags are lower-case kebab-case (braided-round-cord). Sizes in mm only when the shop states them.
  5. A trim's parts follow the catalogue: a drawcord has Cord, Ends, Exit, Stopper; in each part's note write what is visible: material, colour, finish, design (e.g. metal · gun black · matt · flat edge, rounded body).
  6. Each art is its own entry: its side, its position, its graphic, font and technique. dtm: true when the art is the fabric's colour (tone-on-tone).
  7. photos_to_delete: suggest photos that add nothing (duplicates, no product, unrelated). They are only suggestions: nothing is deleted.
  8. Answer with one JSON block exactly in the answer format, every job of this round in it, nothing else in the block.
